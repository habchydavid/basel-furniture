The house

Named for the ear of wheat

Sonbol Cafe sits in the centre of Deir El Ahmar, a farming town 22 km north-west of Baalbek where the plain gives way to the hills. Everything we cook belongs to that plain.

The kitchen

Lebanese, with a wide door

Mezze and charcoal-grilled farrouj next to pizza, pasta and burgers — the menu people in the village actually order from, cooked properly.

The room

Modern, not formal

Built for long tables and loud evenings. Arguileh on the terrace, a full bar, and space for a family of twelve without rearranging the place.

The region

Wine country

We pour local Bekaa wine, including Couvent Rouge, grown on some of the highest vineyards in the Middle East — a short drive from the door.
